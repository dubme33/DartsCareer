# Darts Career 1.5.2

## Custom Scoreboard Colours

Open More → Scoreboard colours in your career hub. Pick a colour or enter a HEX code for each circuit, including your own, and preview it before saving. Colours apply in both regular matches and TV mode, and are stored with each career. Restore the default for any circuit at any time.

## Choose Rankings for Custom Tournaments

Open a ranking event in the tournament editor and choose Selected rankings. Each selected built-in or custom ranking receives the full eligible prize money. Your choices carry over when you duplicate or export the tournament.

## European Tour Reserve Lists in the Calendar

Open Reserve list next to a European Tour event to see available reserves and replacements. Existing saves can recover a missing list from recorded Tour Card qualifier results. When a seed withdraws before the draw, the highest-ranked eligible entrant moves into the seeded round and the reserve starts in round one.

## Support for the Community 2015 Calendar

Tournament packs now support Civic's 66-event 2015 calendar, including a split Premier League season, World Cup team ties and events that cross into the next calendar year. Load the calendar JSON from Tournament packs in the tournament editor.

## Smoother 3D Match Rendering

Dart shaders are prepared before the first throw. The renderer reuses shadows during camera moves and refreshes them at a lower rate during dart motion, with updates at impact and when the dart settles. In-venue TV screens update a smaller score texture.

## Easier-to-Follow TV Throws

Live dart flight in TV mode lasts 20% longer at the same playback speed. The score appears when the dart reaches the board.

## More Natural Barrel Width in 3D Close-Ups

A longer camera lens reduces perspective distortion in board close-ups and replays, so slim barrels look closer to their selected diameter.

## Stable Darts After Impact

After settling briefly, a landed dart holds its final position and entry angle. Resting darts stay stable when another dart lands nearby or a slow-motion replay plays.
