# Darts Career 1.5.2

## Custom scoreboard colours

Open **More → Scoreboard colours** in the career hub. Choose a circuit or a major, then pick a colour or enter a HEX code. Preview the colour before saving, or restore the default for the selected circuit.

The setting covers regular and TV scoreboards, including spectated matches and custom circuits. Colours are saved separately with each career and travel with its exported save.

## Prize money rankings for custom tournaments

In the tournament editor, enable **Ranking event**, choose **Selected rankings** and tick the rankings that should receive the prize money. Built-in financial rankings and custom tour rankings are available. Each selected table receives the full eligible gross prize. The existing rules option retains the tournament's automatic ranking behavior. Choices are kept in career saves, tournament packs and duplicates.
