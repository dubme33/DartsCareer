# Darts Career 1.5.1 - Match Venues, Career Tools & Planned Absences

## 🎯 Television Stages and Floor Tournament Halls

Matches can take place on a television stage or in a floor tournament hall with 16 numbered stations. Let the tournament choose its venue or select one yourself. Switch between the venue view and a board close-up; aiming or throwing returns the camera to the board. Floor ambience has its own on/off setting.

## 🖼️ Sharper Boards and Matching Floor Stations

Higher-resolution branding, backdrop artwork and live score monitors improve the stage view. Neighbouring floor stations use the same board and surround design as the active target, including community mods, with consistent perspective and lighting. More stations and revised framing give the hall a natural continuation.

## 🎨 TV Walls Match Each Tournament

Stage walls, decorative bands and score monitors follow the tournament scoreboard palette, including custom editor colours. The centre and sides now form one flat wall with a continuous pattern, without the vertical strips beside the old centre panel. The black surround keeps the target distinct from the background.

## 📅 Declare Absences Before an Event

Open Absences in the calendar to choose a player, a reason and an announcement date. Future announcements can be cancelled; announced absences are binding. AI players also announce withdrawals ahead of events, normally three days before the tournament or qualifier. Declared absences do not count as played defeats or earn participation prizes. Decisions and notifications are retained in career saves.

## 🎟️ European Tour Withdrawals Before and After Qualifying

Withdrawals before the Tour Card qualifier update automatic places through the entry rankings. Later replacements use the saved list of final-round qualifier losers. Before the main draw, eligible entrants move up into vacant seed positions while reserves start in round one. Replacements can be confirmed while regional qualifiers remain pending, without playing those qualifiers early.

## 🩹 Stable Draws After Injuries

A new injury after a European Tour draw replaces the unavailable entrant while preserving existing withdrawals and healthy pairings. Walkovers no longer alter player ratings or start a match against an injured opponent. Qualifier endings and World Youth group walkovers also progress correctly.

## 🛠️ Nine Grip Sections and 24 Free-Placement Regions

Use nine smaller grip sections on every barrel shape, or switch to up to 24 freely placed regions with start and end positions in millimetres. Duplicate regions and overlap patterns, such as rings and longitudinal cuts. Regions scale with barrel length, switching back restores the previous nine-section choices, and existing grip purchases remain valid.

## 📏 Barrel Diameters and Refined Pixelgrip

Set barrel diameter from 5.0 to 9.0 mm in 0.1 mm steps in personal customisation and the AI dart editor. Restore the original diameter for a barrel shape at any time. Pixelgrip has a more distinct machined pattern, and existing setups retain their previous dimensions unless changed.

## 🔩 More Point Styles

Choose from ten point styles, including front or rear rings, spiral, diamond, fluted and dual-grip designs alongside the existing smooth, ringed, etched and tapered options. Point details carry through the shop preview, AI dart editor and the darts used in matches.

## 🖌️ Fit Your Artwork and Download Flight Templates

Fit, fill, move, zoom and rotate imported flight artwork before applying it. Download PNG or SVG templates for a single flight wing, prepare your design and import it with the Prepared template option. The same artwork workflow is available for personal and AI dart setups.

## 💾 Three Named Dart Sets with Thumbnails

Save three personal dart sets with your own names and switch between them without rebuilding the setup. Sets include grip regions, barrel dimensions, points, shafts, flights, colours and flight artwork. Each slot shows a thumbnail and dimensions, and sets remain separate for each career through saves and JSON exports.

## 🗂️ Ten Careers and Automatic Backups

The career panel keeps up to ten separate careers with player, date and progress previews, plus three backups for each slot. Existing saves migrate into the panel, and JSON imports create another slot. Restore an earlier backup while retaining the current state as a safety copy. Slots and backups are stored in the current browser.

## 🎯 Bob's 27, Around the Clock and Checkout Challenges

Play three short practice modes using your current darts and the regular throwing system. Checkout challenges have three difficulty levels, with personal records and recent results saved per career. Practice sessions can be resumed and leave the career date, stamina, XP and match statistics unchanged.

## 👥 Two Players on One Device

Play a separate local match with two people taking turns manually. Choose database players or guests, give each player their own darts, and select 301 or 501 with double-out and a leg target. Local results stay outside the career; the match lasts for the current page session.

## 🌍 Explore Your Career on the World Map

See countries where you have competed, with appearances, wins and gross prize money. Open country details and filter by season or qualifiers. New results retain the tournament location from the day it was played, while older appearances are reconstructed from the history available in the save.

## 🪪 Download a Career Card

Download a PNG card with your player photo, Order of Merit position, season average, achievements and current darts. The card is available from the start of a career and uses the recorded statistics available at the time. Preview it from your profile and save it for sharing.

## 🎱 A Draw Ceremony for UK Open

Reveal UK Open and British Open pairings one player at a time, follow completed pairs and see who could still become your opponent. Entry groups control when players join, while the draw itself uses one unseeded pool. Reveal the whole draw at once or continue a saved ceremony without drawing the pairs again.

## 🧩 Combine Rankings and Qualifier Results

Split a built-in qualification route between several sources, or use tournament and qualifier results in your own entry rules. For example, allocate five Asian places to Asian Tour leaders and two to Asian Championship results. Sources must finish before the target event, duplicate places pass down, and the rules travel with tournament packs.

## 🏆 Group Wins Across Your Own Tour

Custom tours can count their titles together, such as Asian Tour ×8, in career achievements. Individual victories, tournament names and years remain available in the results history. The Group titles setting is retained in career saves and shared calendar packs.

## 🔮 Explore What-If Qualification Scenarios

Add up to eight hypothetical results for your player and rivals to explore future ranking positions. The calculator includes eligible prize money, known money due to expire and custom tour prizes. It leaves the real career unchanged and presents a projection rather than a confirmed qualification place.

## 🔎 Diagnose Mods Before Importing

Scan an active database or a ZIP or mod.json file for player, tournament, prize and qualification problems. Filter findings, download a JSON report and open supported active-career issues in the editor. File reports identify the relevant mod entry, and scanning applies no changes to the career.

## 📖 A Clearer Start Screen and Four-Language Guides

The start screen explains the game and provides separate shortcuts for choosing an existing player, creating a player and opening the guide. Read playing instructions in English, Polish, German or Dutch. Long player names remain visible, and the changelog can be expanded on mobile without stretching the start forms.

## 🌐 Correct Player Nationalities

Aaron Hardy is Austrian and Kaya Baysal represents Türkiye. British women use their constituent countries, such as England, Scotland and Wales, rather than a shared Great Britain entry. Corrections cover the base database and matching profiles when community mods are used.

## 📦 Imported Darts for Your Career Player

A matching imported dart setup can now reach your active career player as well as AI players. Starting a career with an existing player also keeps their imported dart appearance. Player skills, results and finances remain separate from visual dart imports.

## 🎙️ Meet Your Match Caller

After walk-ons, a short presentation card identifies the caller assigned to the match. It follows the selected caller and available community recordings, with translated interface labels and no extra delay before throwing.

## ⚡ Faster Tour Event Simulation

Reduced repeated historical-title searches and large-bracket work make European Tour, Challenge Tour and Development Tour simulation more responsive. Tournament draws, match results, prizes and player development retain the existing sporting rules.

## 🚀 Quicker Rankings, Calendar and Editors

Rankings, calendar lists and editor screens refresh faster in large careers. Repeated database scans and unnecessary table layout work have been reduced. Venue views keep sharper desktop textures and a smaller mobile rendering budget, while staying idle when nothing changes.

## 🍪 Analytics Only After Your Choice

A consent banner lets you allow statistics or keep only essential storage, and the footer lets you change that choice later. Google Analytics loads after consent and stops when permission is withdrawn. Privacy information has been updated, while advertising remains disabled.
