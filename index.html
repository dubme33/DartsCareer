📢 What's New / Changelog

v1.3.1 · Career Stability & Spectator Update
Save & Calendar 💾 Tournaments No Longer Disappear

Loaded careers now safely restore major events, Continental Tours and their qualification history. Saves created with a mod remain playable without reloading that mod, without empty qualifier fields or all-BYE draws.

Spectator Mode 🎵 AI Walk-ons, Match Speed & Pause

Watched AI matches now begin with introductions and walk-on music for both players. A slower x1 pace gives the caller time to finish, while x2, x4, x8 and x16 controls plus a true pause/resume button let you watch at your preferred speed.

World Series Finals 🌍 Complete and Correct Last 32

The finals field now contains all 32 players: the Top 24 World Series ranking players, four further Order of Merit players and four qualifiers from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier.

Player Profiles 🏆 Full Career Title History

Player profiles now show every title won across the entire saved career, grouped by tournament with the number of victories instead of displaying only the current season.

Career Balance 🔋 More Stamina, Softer Veteran Decline

Advancing a day restores five more stamina than before. Automatic age decline now starts after 43 and increases gradually, while veteran match results no longer apply an additional hidden penalty to losses.

Newgens 🌱 Richer, Country-Accurate Names

Newgens use much larger pools of locally appropriate first and last names. Repeated identities are reduced, and major darts nations are represented far more often than tiny or exotic countries.

Player Database 🇮🇪 Mickey Mansell Represents Ireland

Mickey Mansell's nationality is corrected to Ireland in the default database, modded careers and existing save files.

v1.3.0 · Tour Cards & Qualification Overhaul
PDC Tour Cards 🪪 A Real Two-Year Tour Card System

The opening Top 64 of the Order of Merit receive PDC Tour Cards, with a further 64 earned through the fictional Pro Card Trials. Cards last two seasons, are visible in rankings and player profiles, and control access to ProTour events and card-holder qualifiers.

Continental Tour 🌍 Authentic 48-Player Qualification

Every Continental Tour now fields 16 OOM seeds, 16 ProTour qualifiers, 10 Tour Card Holder Qualifiers, four host-nation qualifiers, one Nordic & Baltic qualifier and one Eastern Europe qualifier. Regional qualifiers exclude Tour Card holders and can be watched or simulated.

World Series 🎟️ Tour Card Finals Qualifier

Global Masters Finals retain their 24 World Series ranking places and four OOM places, while the final four places now come strictly from a qualifier open only to eligible PDC Tour Card holders.

World Championship 🏆 A Full 128-Player Qualification Field

The World Championship now combines the Top 80 OOM with structured regional, youth, women’s and non-Tour Card routes to create a unique 128-player field without duplicate entries.

Rankings 📊 Authentic Two-Year Order of Merit

The main Order of Merit uses ranking prize money over a rolling two-year window. Opening rankings use the verified 2026 baseline, while results earned in career mode replace expiring money naturally.

Career & Tournaments 👀 Faster Progress, Better Tournament Control

After elimination you can simulate an entire remaining tournament at once, or watch AI matches live from the bracket. Players Championship payouts, stamina recovery and costs have also been rebalanced.

Match & AI 🎯 Safer Turns and Smarter Setups

Rapid clicking can no longer throw more than three darts in a visit. AI checkout and setup choices now use practical alternatives such as 19s, 18s, 17s and Bull routes when they preserve a finish.

v1.2.6 · Authentic Formats & New Talent
World Grand Prix 🎯 Correct Set Format

World Grand Prix matches now use best of 3 sets in round one, best of 5 in round two and the quarter-finals, best of 9 in the semi-finals, and best of 11 in the final. Every set remains first to 3 legs under Double-In, Double-Out rules.

Career Balance 📈 Softer Veteran Decline

Veterans aged 41+ now improve at 85% of the normal rate and decline at 110%, preventing established players from losing too much overall too quickly. Young AI players retain their 65% growth bonus, while young career players receive a more measured 25% bonus.

Newgens 🌱 Wider Debut Age Range

New fictional players can now enter the game aged 17–35, allowing for more believable late bloomers alongside young prospects.

v1.2.5 · Prize Money & Match Pace
Premier League 💷 League-Phase Prize Money

Players finishing fifth to eighth in the Premier League now receive £95k, £90k, £85k and £80k respectively. These payments go to the career wallet only and do not affect any ranking.

European Championship 🏆 Reworked Prize Fund

The European Championship now awards £150k to the winner, £80k to the runner-up, then £50k, £35k, £20k and £7.5k through to the last 32. All prizes count towards the main Order of Merit.

AI 🎯 Quicker Throws

AI players now take 0.65 seconds between individual darts, making matches flow a little faster without removing the feel of a real turn.

v1.2.4 · Deeper Career, Better Darts
Career Balance 📈 Slower, Capped Progression

Random events and training now produce steadier growth. Training costs more stamina, gives less progress, is limited to two sessions per week, and every stat is capped at 100. Age now matters too: under-25s develop faster, 25–40s progress normally, and 41+ players improve more slowly while declining faster.

Major Formats 🏆 Authentic Grand Slam & World Championship Rules

The Grand Slam now uses sixteen groups of three before a 16-player knockout, while the World Championship applies its deciding-set, win-by-two and sudden-death rules from round one.

Rankings 💶 European Tour Order of Merit

European Championship qualification now comes from a season-long European Tour Order of Merit. The ProTour ranking has also been corrected to use a rolling 52-week window.

AI & Simulation 🎯 More Volatile, More Human Darts

AI players can produce exceptional 110+ averages, including capable underdogs on a great day. Players Championship and European Tour events now create more believable upsets, hot streaks and off-days, with more natural throw timing.

Players Championship 🚫 Top Players Can Skip Floor Events

Top-16 AI players now occasionally withdraw from Players Championship events, allowing the next eligible players in the Order of Merit to take their places.

World Cup 🌍 Fairer Draws & Real Teams

World Cup seeding now produces balanced bracket halves. The database also adds Shane Sawyer for the Bahamas alongside Rashad Sweeting, while preserving World Cup-only eligibility.

Database 🪪 Safer Real-Name Careers

Starting a career as a real player can no longer create an AI duplicate. Corrected player nationalities and identity mappings also apply consistently with or without a real-names mod.

Save System 👋 Retirement-Safe Mods & Draws

Retired AI players cannot be restored by a mod or a saved draw. Older saves are migrated safely, replacing unavailable entrants before they can generate NaN averages or invalid 6–0 results.

v1.2.3 · Smarter Tours & Career Logic
World Series 🌎 Top 14 Invitation Draw

Each World Series event now draws its eight PDC invitations exclusively from the current Top 14 Order of Merit, while preserving complete local fields.

Save System 🗽 Legacy US Masters Repair

Older saves using “US Masters” are automatically migrated to the current USA World Series event without losing tournament history or an active draw.

Career 👋 Reliable AI Retirements

AI players now consider retirement from age 46 at 2.5%, rising by 2.5 percentage points per year. Retired players can no longer reappear after loading a save.

AI 🎯 Smarter High-Score Setups

AI players use 18s, 19s and the outer bull in selected high-score routes to leave practical 160–170 finishes instead of always starting on T20.

v1.2.2 · Tournament Balance & Stability
Balance 🎲 More Tournament Upsets

Tournament simulations now produce more believable surprises. Underdogs can find a hot streak, while favourites can occasionally have an off weekend.

Masters 🏆 Retirement-Safe Masters Fields

All World Series events, the Finals Qualifier and the Global Masters Finals now replace unavailable retired players to keep every draw complete.

Career 🛡️ Long Career Stability

Incomplete opening Masters draws in older saves are automatically rebuilt with available replacements, so your career can always continue.

Database 🇦🇺 Player Data Correction

Corrected Adam Leek's nationality to Australia in new and existing careers, including games using a mod.

v1.2.1 · World Cup & Save Fixes
World Cup 🏆 World Cup of Darts Tournament Tracker

Follow every World Cup of Darts group and knockout round as it happens, simulate the next stage, and review complete match scores in calendar history.

Save System 💾 Reliable World Cup Saves

Fixed saving and loading careers during the World Cup, including downloaded .JSON save files.

Records 🎯 Personal Bests Corrected

Career Chronicle now adds highest averages and checkouts only when you truly beat your previous record.

v1.2.0 · Global Career Expansion
Tournament 🌍 Nations Cup Integration

Represent your country in a 40-nation doubles event: group stage, knockout bracket, four seeded teams and alternating throws.

Qualification 🎟️ International Qualification Pathways

Regional Asian, Nordic & Baltic, Latin American/Caribbean and African routes now decide the final Nations Cup places.

Feature 📬 National Team Selection Mail

Receive messages when you make your country's top two, enter a qualifier or see the confirmed Nations Cup field.

Profiles 👤 Detailed Player Profiles

Open any ranked player to view their photo, age, OVR, rankings, season results, major successes and best seasonal average.

Career 🎮 Take Over Any Database Player

Begin a full career with any player from the database. Your chosen pro is removed from the AI opponent pool.

Career 🔥 Rivalries & Head-to-Head

Track your biggest official rivalries, H2H records, important clashes and streaks. Strong H2H form can affect match performance.

Career 📖 Career Chronicle

Relive trophies, genuine personal records, new rivalries and season summaries in a dedicated career timeline.

Career 🎂 Ageing, Retirements & Newgens

Players now age, decline after 45 and can retire at season's end. New fictional talents arrive every 1 January.

Tournament 🎯 Continental Tour Qualifiers

Each 48-player Continental Tour now has Top 16 OOM seeds, 16 ProTour qualifiers and 16 winners from a 128-player qualifier.

Bug Fix 💷 Continental Tour Prize Money

Corrected payouts for every stage, from £2,000 in round one to £35,000 for the champion.

Tournament ✈️ Global Masters Circuit

Six non-ranking invitational events bring eight PDC stars and eight regional players together across the world.

Rankings 📊 Global Masters Finals

Earn circuit points, qualify through the new standings and compete in a 32-player Amsterdam Finals event.

Database 🌎 Expanded International Player Pool

Added more players and nations for regional events and national-team qualifiers. Non-Tour Card players start on £0 OOM.

Bug Fix 📈 Highest Average Records

Career and season records now update only after a genuine new best average, including simulated AI matches.

Achievement 🏆 Nations Cup Champion

A new Trophy Room achievement awaits players who lift the Nations Cup for their country.

UI 🧩 Player Creation Screen Refresh

The database-player quick start is now compact, while the changelog stays aligned with the full creation panel.

Localization 🌐 Full Language Coverage

All new career, national-team and Global Masters content is available in Polish, English, German and Dutch.
