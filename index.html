📢 What's New / Changelog

v1.4.7 · Dart Customisation, Match Flow & Long-Career Performance
Match Start 🎯 Throw for the Bull Before Walk-ons

Each player throws one dart before the entrances; the dart closer to the centre starts the match, even if neither reaches the bull or outer bull. Ties reverse the order and a fallen dart is thrown again. The first dart stays in the board for the second throw unless it hits the exact centre. Choose the regular 2D or closer 3D board view; the result also controls the opening leg in watched and simulated matches.

Dart Physics 🏹 Rare Robin Hood Darts

A very rare dart can lodge in the flight of one already in the board. It scores nothing and appears in the 3D presentation. Robin Hoods are possible only with a separate shaft and flight, never with integrated systems.

Match Events 🎭 Rarer Moments, Stage-Only Crowd Events and an Off Switch

Match decisions occur less often and can be disabled completely in Options. Crowd-related moments appear only at stage events, not Players Championship or floor qualifiers. A pending moment can no longer block the screen after a match or change the score when a simulated leg finishes.

Press Conferences 🎙️ Pre-Tournament and Post-Win Questions

Majors have a press conference before your first match; majors and European Tour events have one after your wins, never after a loss. Eight journalists draw from 96 questions about form, opponents, match results and verified head-to-head records. Four replies can change professionalism and media presence, with reactions that can vary even for the same answer.

Premier League 📊 Balanced Opening Pairs and a Live League Table

Across the regular nights, each player meets every rival twice in an opening quarter-final; nights 8 and 16 provide the extra pairings. The pre-match table now includes results from earlier matches that night, making the stakes clear even on the final night.

World Cup Editor 🌍 Create and Edit National Teams

Choose a country's two representatives, including eligible players outside its usual leading pair, or replace a nation in a future World Cup field. Saved selections feed the tournament and its qualifiers while an edition already under way keeps its existing teams.

Tournament Editor 📦 Share Tournament Packs

Export your calendar, event settings and linked qualifiers to one JSON file. Preview a shared pack before importing it and choose whether to replace or skip each matching tournament. Career match results are not included or overwritten.

Dart Customisation 🎨 Put Your Own Artwork on Flights

Import a PNG design for standard flights or integrated systems. Your artwork appears on the dart model and stays with the saved set.

Dart Customisation ⚙️ More Grips, Patterns, Weights and Barrel Sizes

Choose different purchased grips for the front, middle and rear of the barrel, along with more flight and integrated-system patterns. Set a barrel length from 35 to 55 mm and a dart weight from 12 to 40 g. These appearance choices do not change accuracy or player attributes.

Mods 🍎 More Reliable ZIP Mods on Mac

The importer finds mod.json inside a folder in a ZIP, ignores macOS metadata files, and refuses to report success when a package has no supported data. Remembered mods are reapplied after loading older careers so real names remain active.

Tournament Flow ➡️ BYE Progression and Translation

A first-round BYE now closes the old bracket before moving to the next round, so the first playable match starts without extra bracket screens. The BYE message follows the selected game language.

Tutorial 🎓 Updated Career Tutorial

Six new chapters explain tour entry routes, TV presentation, match incidents, player identity, work and press conferences, and the editors. They include shortcuts to relevant screens in all four languages. Existing careers keep their completed chapters and see a badge for new material.

Performance ⚡ Smoother Long Careers and Smaller Saves

Daily career updates avoid repeated hub rebuilding, while long match histories use a lossless, compact key pool in exported saves. Career screens and archives do less unnecessary copying and rendering as seasons accumulate, without discarding results or statistics.

3D Matches 🚀 Faster Darts and Lighter Match Rendering

Normal 3D darts reach the board sooner while slow-motion replays keep their timing. The TV camera now frames the board a little closer in front and angled views. Common barrel grips use a lighter mesh with the same fine axial detail, and unchanged flags and scoreboard elements no longer rebuild on every throw. Scoring and accuracy are unaffected.

v1.4.6 · Walk-on Atmosphere, Match Stories & Flexible Viewing
Career Choice 💼 Combine Work with Darts or Go Full-Time

Choose your career focus at the start or change it later in Options. A job pays £2,000 each month but reduces effective energy and professionalism by 20; focusing on darts costs £2,000 each month without those penalties. The modifiers reverse immediately when you switch.

Walk-on Arena ✨ Stage Lights, Smoke and LED Screens

Player introductions now take place against a stage-inspired backdrop with moving lights, smoke and LED panels. The panels adjust long player names so full surnames remain visible.

Walk-on Viewing ⛶ Optional Full-Screen Introductions

A button on the player card can show both entrances full screen, giving the cards and arena more room. Full screen closes when the introductions finish or are skipped.

Tournament Presentation 🏆 Match Stakes, Rivalries and Premier League Table

Introductions now explain what is at stake in the match and show head-to-head context. Rival records retain the five most recent meetings and the latest final, including after saving and loading. Premier League introductions also show the current league table.

Board View 🎯 Closer Board in Regular 3D Matches

The regular 3D camera now frames the scoring fields more closely, so players who prefer it do not need TV mode to see the board clearly. The TV establishing shot keeps its wider presentation.

Board Geometry 🕸️ Wires Stay Aligned in Side Views

The board spider and ring wires were aligned with the scoring fields, including the angled camera, close-ups and replays. Wires no longer appear to run outside their intended sections.

Display Options 📐 Set Board and Interface Size Separately

Choose Compact, Standard or Large independently for the board and match controls, with a live layout preview in Options. The choices apply to regular and TV matches, persist in the browser and do not alter dart accuracy or results.

Dart Animation 🚀 Smoother, Configurable 3D Dart Flight

Dart flight now uses smoother frame timing on high-refresh displays. By default the dart stays visible in flight even if Windows animations are disabled; Options can instead follow the system motion preference or turn the flight animation off.

TV Layout 💻 Controls and Statistics Fit Laptop Screens

The TV sidebar measures its available space so quick throws and aiming controls can scroll without covering live statistics or the throw buttons on shorter laptop displays.

TV Camera 📷 Camera Resets After a Leg Without Replays

When replays are off, the TV camera returns to its normal board view as the next leg begins instead of staying zoomed in through the opening visit. A live attempt at a maximum still gets its normal camera attention.

Career Hub ⚡ Readiness Shows Current Preparation

The readiness card now reads the player's actual current preparation, including changes from the training base, rather than repeatedly displaying 70/100. Its advice updates with the new value.

Spectator Mode 👁 Watch World Cup and Grand Slam Group Matches

You can watch eligible World Cup matches and Grand Slam group games through spectator mode. The watched result and both players' statistics are kept when the tournament continues, without simulating the same match again.

Tournament Editor 🗑️ Deleting One Event Keeps the Others

Deleting a tournament removes only that event and its own linked qualifiers. Unrelated events remain in the calendar, including large edited schedules that previously collapsed to qualifiers alone.

Tournament Editor 🗺️ Edit Qualifiers for Existing Events

Custom qualification routes can now target built-in tournaments as well as newly created ones. Changing a European Tour event's host country also updates its host-nation qualifier and refreshes an unplayed host pool, without rebuilding the entire event.

Match Events 🎲 21 Situations During Your Matches

Crowd reactions, equipment issues, pauses, officials and opponent behaviour can interrupt a visit with a short decision. Each choice can have several outcomes, temporarily affecting scoring or doubles and sometimes changing professionalism or media presence. Pending decisions survive a save and cannot be bypassed by another throw method.

Career Stories 🎤 Match Decisions Can Return Later

Selected match moments can become continuing stories. A related question may appear in a later interview; the next meeting with that opponent can recall the incident during the walk-on and opening visit. Conduct can also shift the next regular-sponsor offers. Each follow-up is recorded and resolved once.

v1.4.5 · Player Identity, TV Controls & Tournament Fixes
Tour Attendance 📅 Challenge & Development Tour Absences

Like Players Championship entrants, the top 20 eligible players in each secondary tour can now skip an event with a 30% chance. Challenge Tour and Development Tour use their own rankings, and the career player is exempt. Absences are remembered for that event so reopening the draw does not reroll attendance.

Exceptional Matches 🔥 Rare 115–125 Match Averages

Strong AI players can occasionally produce exceptional matches averaging 115–125 in both played and quickly simulated matches. These peaks depend on ability, form and consistency, remain rare and survive saving and loading an ongoing match.

AI Match Results 🎯 Scores and Averages Tell the Same Story

AI results now come from shared leg-by-leg 501 races, with scores and averages calculated from the same performance. Large average deficits rarely produce close scores or dominant wins. A player with a lower average can still win through timely finishes, while set matches, deciding legs and bounce-outs follow the same model.

Career Balance 📈 Healthier Young and Low-Rated Players

Young AI players now have a more restrained development bonus and can lose rating after poor results, limiting inflation at the top of the rankings. Rating losses below 65 OVR are softened, helping weaker players remain competitive over longer careers.

Major Tournaments 🏆 Meaningful Major-Tournament Upsets

Major events give underdogs a credible chance while keeping favourites more likely to win. Players around 10 OVR below their opponent win roughly one third of shorter major matches; longer World Championship matches and larger rating gaps favour the stronger player more. Renamed mod tournaments retain the correct balance.

Walk-on Presentation 🚶 Player Cards with Photos and Tournament Honours

Walk-ons now introduce each player with a broadcast-style card showing their portrait, age, nickname, seeding and tournament history, including their best result and appearances. Available player photos are used, with initials as a fallback. Cards adapt to the screen and use the active tournament’s presentation.

National Identity 🌍 Reliable Country Flags on Walk-on Cards

Player introductions now display the flag of the country they represent. Local flag images also work on Windows, where flag emoji may appear as letters. The former country-name label has been replaced by the player’s nickname, avoiding untranslated country names in English walk-ons.

Player Nicknames 💬 Choose, Edit and Display Darter Nicknames

Choose a nickname when creating your player and change it later in Options. The player editor can also edit darter nicknames, and nicknames appear in walk-ons and the profiles opened by clicking player names. Real nicknames have been matched to 180 existing players; custom nicknames remain intact in saves and mods.

Final Score Layout 🏁 Double-Digit Scores and Long Names Fit

The TV final-score screen now gives both score numbers enough room and adapts player-name sizes and spacing. Results such as 11–16 and long surnames remain readable alongside portraits, without overlapping the score or cutting into the opposite player’s panel.

TV Director Options 🎬 Control Replay and Statistics Frequency

Director Options are available from the More menu and the TV toolbar. Set replays and automatic statistics independently to Off, Rare, Normal or Often for both playing and spectating. Preferences are saved, and changing them during a match keeps play moving. More frequent replays can also highlight big 140+ visits.

World News Options 📰 Hide Player Form-Change News

Options now let you hide Darts World News messages about rises and falls in player form. Other news stays visible, and player form continues to change normally in the background.

Qualifier Notifications 🔔 European Tour Alerts Only When You Take Part

European Tour qualification-day messages and invitations now appear only when the career player can take part in that qualifier, matching the attendance-aware behaviour of Challenge Tour and Development Tour. Viewing the calendar or qualification preview no longer freezes a future qualification list.

Regular Sponsors 🤝 Veyronix Technologies Joins the Sponsor Pool

Veyronix Technologies is now available as a regular sponsor, using its supplied Veyronix logo. It can appear in sponsor offers both with and without a mod, and remains available when a mod replaces other regular sponsors.

Tournament Editor 🗺️ Qualification by Represented Country

Tournament qualification routes can now reserve places for players representing a chosen country, using a selected ranking to decide their order. Multiple country quotas can be combined with other routes. Duplicate qualifiers roll down within the same country, normal eligibility rules still apply, and previews match the actual draw.

Grand Slam Qualification ✅ Correct Grand Slam Qualifiers Across Seasons

The direct Grand Slam field now stays up to date until its Tour Card Holder Qualifier actually starts. Later major finalists are included, unfinished events do not invent finalists, and previous-season qualifier results cannot award new places. The final eight places go to actual qualifier winners, while started and completed qualification results remain fixed.

Save Compatibility 💾 Preserve Qualified Players After Reloading

Loading a career now correctly distinguishes original players from mod replacements even after the roster changes order. Legitimate player identities, Tour Cards, rankings and qualification references are preserved, preventing qualified players from being removed or replaced when the save reloads.

Dart Equipment 🛒 Integrated Flights and Refined Dart Models

The dart shop now offers solid and transparent integrated shaft-and-flight systems with a shared colour. The shop preview and match use the same model, with shaped barrels, detailed grips, slender shafts, thin flight wings and selectable point and shaft lengths. Darts sit at a more natural depth in the board, and existing equipment choices remain compatible.

Bounce-out Replays ⏪ Replay the Correct Dart and Board State

Bounce-out highlights now remember the darts already embedded when the rebound happened and replay the correct throw. Later darts and a cleared board no longer change the scene, and the replay does not incorrectly include the whole visit.

Broadcast Statistics 📊 Accurate Visit Totals and Readable TV Cards

Fast simulation now preserves 100–139 and 140–179 visit counts for both players, including checkout visits, so live TV statistics remain correct after simulation. Compact cards sit beside the board, while longer cards accommodate long names and aligned values. Spectated matches retain their actual statistics when the tournament processes the result.

Broadcast Flow ⏱️ Smoother TV Matches, Replays and Match Finishes

TV play and fast AI spectating recover correctly after statistics cards, replays, pauses and mode changes. Pending actions resume once, preventing stalled turns or duplicate match completion. Caller announcements, the final replay, the score presentation and stage celebration music stay in order, and leaving spectator mode stops pending throws.

Board and Scoreboard 🎯 Earlier Nine-Darter Indicators and Clearer Board Wires

The TV nine-darter indicator now appears after an eligible opening visit, even before a checkout route is available, and disappears when a perfect leg becomes impossible. Scoreboard spacing and the board surround fit have been refined. Bull sizing and board wires are clearer on both the default board and the modded Winmau Blade X.

World Masters opening fields no longer create unintended BYEs or seed unqualified players. Older careers with incomplete statistics can finish and record Challenge Tour victories safely. v1.4.2 · Match Reports, Career Events & Gameplay Balance
Match Reports 📋 Practical Post-Match Reports

Singles matches now show your average, first-nine average, checkout accuracy, 180s, accuracy on each double, missed match darts and checkouts under pressure. Compare your performance with up to ten previous matches and receive specific training advice. Both your player and AI opponents can suffer injuries or experience temporary rises and drops in form. Injured players cannot train or enter tournaments until their full recovery period ends, and their health status and recovery date are shown in the career.

Career Investments 🏫 Darts Academy & Pub Ownership

Buy a darts academy for an additional 5% chance of generating an extra prospect from your country, or invest in a pub that earns passive monthly income. Both investments are available through career infrastructure and persist in your save.

Hire a career manager to boost professionalism or a social media manager to boost media presence. Each role has three tiers, with a signing fee and monthly salary. These two manager roles do not count towards the staff-member limit.

Tournament Watching 📺 Choose Which Events to Follow

Career options let you choose which tournaments to follow round by round on their scheduled day. Events you do not enter and have not selected for watching can be simulated in the background; viewing preferences do not withdraw your player from an event.

Match Mechanics ↘ Bounce-Outs for Players & AI

Rare bounce-outs score zero points and consume the dart without a rethrow. The base risk is 0.3%, with a small increase for tightly grouped darts. The same rules apply to you and AI, equipment has no effect, and bounce-outs can be disabled in the options.

Player Development 📈 Results Reward Tournament Prestige

Overall growth from tournament results now depends more strongly on event prestige. Challenge Tour and Development Tour results award less development, while important tournaments offer slightly greater rewards. AI season development also accounts for the prestige of the events played.

Favourite Doubles 🎯 Three Ranked Favourite Doubles

Choose three different favourite doubles when creating or editing your player. The first, second and third favourites add 5, 3 and 1 percentage points respectively to the chance of hitting those doubles. Your selections are retained in the career save.

If your player enters the UK Open in a later round, use “Simulate until my match” to advance through the earlier rounds automatically. Simulation stops as soon as your player joins the draw, ready for their opening match.

PDC Tour Card holders are excluded from Challenge Tour fields, including the opening season. This prevents top-ranked card holders such as Wessel Nijman from incorrectly competing in and winning these events.

Career Titles 🏆 Grouped Secondary-Tour Titles

Challenge Tour and Development Tour event victories now appear as grouped career-title entries with their win counts and winning years, instead of a separate trophy row for every numbered event. Fictional circuit names and modded PDC names follow the same grouping rules.

On Hard difficulty, 30% of your tournament prize money is deducted as tax. Financial statements show the gross prize, tax and net payout, while ranking money retains the full gross amount. The tax applies to future prize payments and does not reduce sponsor income or bonuses.
