v1.3.9 · Tournament Reliability, Results & Training
Grand Slam 🏆 Grand Slam Save Recovery

Incomplete Grand Slam qualifier and main-event fields in older or modded saves are now repaired safely, so the event can be opened or skipped again instead of trapping the career in a broken tournament state.

Top 16 main Order of Merit seeds and Top 16 ProTour entrants now receive an opening-loss payment only in their career wallet, never in a ranking including the European Tour Order of Merit. Live round summaries, completed tournament results and Grand Slam group results now show the flag of each player’s country next to their name.

Training remains blocked on the day of your own tournament, but is now available when you are not in the field — for example, during a Premier League night. The AI event is resolved in the background first, then the training session uses the day as normal.

When a tournament is ready, the “What Now?” panel now offers a second button to skip it and simulate the event in the background. It safely finishes the current draw, preserves results already played and returns the career to the hub without requiring manual matches.

Grand Slam Tour Card Holder Qualifiers now build the correct dynamic knockout bracket from all eligible card holders and award exactly eight places without accidentally using the main event’s group format. v1.3.7 · Match Screen & Training Balance

Larger player photos and a larger dartboard restore the focus on the match. The scoreboard stays visible while scrolling, with a separate phone layout and a scrollable match log.

Touch Controls 👆 Target Tiles & Multiplier Buttons

Choose sectors 1–20, Outer Bull and Bull from large tiles, with separate Single, Double and Treble buttons and a highlighted selection. Returning from either bull target to a numbered sector restores Treble instead of carrying over Single or Double. Touch targets remain at least 44 pixels high.

Throwing Balance 🔥 Consecutive Treble & Double Hits

Hitting the intended treble or double improves the chance of repeating that exact target during the same visit, making 180s and consecutive doubles more achievable for both players and AI. The bonus is capped and ends after a miss, a target change, the visit or the leg; it never carries into the next leg.

Match Log 📝 Simulated Leg Winners in the Log

Career Balance ⚡ Energy-Based Training Efficiency

All five training types now scale XP with energy at the start of the session: 100% energy gives full XP, 80% gives 80%, and 50% gives half. The reduction also applies to equipment, staff and analysis bonuses. Training still costs 20 energy and uses the shared two-session weekly limit. The training screen shows current XP efficiency in all four languages.

Career Flow 📬 No Random Events or Filler Emails

Removed daily random events and all 80 filler email templates. Old saves are cleaned of random mail while preserving important career messages and their read status. Bundled mods no longer contain random events, and importing or restoring an older mod cannot bring them back. Post-match interviews remain available.

v1.3.6 · Smarter AI & European Tour Update
AI Development 📈 Results-Based AI Season Development

Year-end AI rating changes now depend mainly on results compared with expectations for the player's rating, opponents and match format. Exceptional or disappointing seasons can produce changes of up to 10 OVR with enough recorded matches, including declines for younger players. Growth slows at elite ratings to limit inflation above 90. Existing careers start collecting the new data without inventing past results; career-player training is unchanged.

AI Scoring 🎯 Smarter AI Scoring Switches

During a scoring visit, a dart can make the AI treat a treble as obstructed and switch from T20 to T19, then T18 or T17 when needed. Checkout routes and useful setup shots remain protected, and obstructions reset for the next visit. Available in played and watched matches, including fast-simulated AI turns, without making every visit follow the same pattern.

European Tour 🎟️ European Tour Withdrawals & Reserve List

Leading AI players can withdraw before a European Tour draw. Losing finalists from the Tour Card Holder Qualifier form a reserve list ordered by the main Order of Merit. Replacements inherit the withdrawn player's place, including a seeded start in round two. Qualification previews show reserves and replacements, and an inbox message confirms when your player receives a place.

Qualification ⏩ Automatic European Tour Qualifiers

European Tour qualifiers now complete automatically when your player is not in the field, covering Tour Card Holder and regional routes. The game records real match results and builds the reserve list without requiring you to open each new draw or pay travel costs for an event you do not enter.

Travel Costs ✈️ Cheaper European Tour Qualifier Travel

Paid travel to European Tour qualifiers is now roughly half the price of travel to the main event: comfort costs £900 and premium £3,000. Economy remains free, and the stamina and preparation benefits are unchanged. The discount covers every qualification route, with or without a mod.

World Matchplay ⚔️ World Matchplay Tie-Breaks in Every Round

Every round now requires a two-leg winning margin until sudden death, rather than applying the rule only in round one. Sudden death starts at 12–12 in round one, 13–13 in round two, 18–18 in the quarter-finals, 19–19 in the semi-finals and 20–20 in the final. Played, watched and simulated matches use the same thresholds and correctly record the deciding result.

Player Database 📊 William O'Connor & Tom Sykes Rating Update

William O'Connor's base rating is now 83 OVR and Tom Sykes' is 73 OVR. The corrections work with both the default database names and modded names, while preserving development already earned in saved careers.

Player Profiles 🏆 Clearer Career Trophy History

Players Championship and European Tour titles are grouped into series totals, such as Players Championship ×12. Major titles show the tournament name and year of victory instead of merging different editions. Existing trophy totals are retained, while unknown historical years are not guessed.

Career Records 👑 Tournament Champions by Edition

Browse tournament winners across your career, see the defending or last recorded champion and find the players with the most titles in recorded history. Confirmed editions remain available across season changes; older saves recover only results with a known year.

Player Comparison 🆚 Player Comparison & Head-to-Head

Compare two players' ratings, season statistics, career titles and recent form side by side. Their official singles head-to-head record persists across seasons. Estimated statistics and incomplete historical coverage are clearly marked.

Tournament Finances 💷 Tournament Prize Tables & Financial Statements

View prize money by finishing position and see which rankings receive it. Your tournament statement tracks actual prizes, attributable cash bonuses and travel charges to show the event balance. Monthly sponsor income, staff salaries and base upkeep are not allocated to individual events; missing amounts in older saves remain marked as unknown.

Prize Money 🌍 Correct World Series Finals Prize Money

The World Series of Darts Finals now uses its own prize table, with £100,000 for the champion instead of the £30,000 paid for winning a regular World Series event. Regular series events keep their separate payouts.

Career Hub 🎨 Colourful Career Hub & Unique Icons

Career menu tiles now use distinct colours and unique emoji icons, making training, travel, staff, records and other screens easier to recognise without repeated symbols.

v1.3.5 · Career Expansion & AI Balance
AI Development 📉 Slower, Stable AI Rating Growth

AI ratings now move through a much smaller zero-sum transfer after tournament matches, so an upset can no longer create extra OVR in the player pool. Young AI players react only 15% faster in both directions instead of gaining at 1.65× while losing at 0.8×. A soft elite factor slows all rating movement in matches involving players above 85 OVR and preserves the existing hierarchy. Five deterministic two-season stress runs keep the 20th-highest rating below 90 and no more than ten players at 90+. Prize money, the career player's training and development, and ratings already stored in existing saves remain unchanged.

Training Equipment 🛠️ Sequential Upgrades & Equipment Wear

Boards, surrounds and lighting must now be purchased one tier at a time, starting at Tier 1. Each category loses one tier after a full year from its latest purchase, while every upgrade starts a fresh one-year service period. The shop shows the active tier, next wear date and locked upgrade path; an inbox message reports every downgrade. Existing careers keep their equipment and receive a full year before its first wear event.

Career Cosmetics 🏡 Home, Trophy Displays & Personalisation

Spend career earnings on five properties, four trophy displays, five shirt styles and five walk-on productions. Purchases are permanent, can be switched freely after unlocking and build a visible collection value without changing OVR, preparation, popularity, sponsorship income or match results. The active home themes the career hub and Trophy Room, shirts style the player card, and entrance effects run inside the existing walk-on without adding delays. The rebuilt Trophy Room uses trophies actually won in the save, connects available years and prize money from the career chronicle, and lets players feature up to three cups without limiting the full collection. Older careers receive the free starter home, shelf, shirt and entrance automatically.

Career Economy ✈️ Training Base, Travel & Preparation

Preparation is a separate 0–100 career stat outside base OVR. A neutral value of 70 has no effect; extreme values adjust effective scoring and doubles by no more than −3 to +2. Buy a private training base for £100,000 plus first-month upkeep, then independently upgrade its training room, recovery area and video analysis for more than £1 million in total long-term investment. Small bonuses improve preparation, rest-day energy and training XP. Monthly upkeep is paid before sponsor income; insufficient funds suspend bonuses without debt or loss of ownership. Before entering each tournament, choose economy, comfort or premium travel. Paid standards cost more at major and international events, reduce tournament stamina cost by 15% or 30%, and protect preparation. Unaffordable travel automatically falls back to economy, and every charge is applied only once.

Player Traits 🧠 Mental Toughness & Expanded Staff

Mental toughness is a separate 0–100 trait outside base OVR. It slightly limits temporary scoring and doubles penalties in deciding legs and sets, on match darts, at major tournaments and for three darts after a bust or missed closing double, without guaranteeing success. Players gain one starting point per two years after age 18 and per recorded tournament title, each capped at +20; qualifiers do not count. Mental training can develop it further. Three sports psychologists improve that training XP by 5%, 10% or 15%, with signing fees and monthly salaries. Staff capacity is expanded from two to three contracts while keeping one person per role.

Career 💪 Endurance & Consistency

Separate 0–100 traits for career and AI players, excluded from base OVR. Older players start with lower endurance and lose it gradually with age; training can offset the decline. Endurance reduces tournament energy costs and fatigue in long matches. Consistency narrows ordinary form swings without a flat accuracy bonus. Two new training types share the existing weekly limit, cost one day and 20 energy, and gain one point per 100 XP. Official singles matches grant small weekly-capped progress. Old saves gain age-based traits without changing trained ratings.

Career 🤝 Player Staff

Choose from eighteen specialists across scoring, doubles, fitness, psychology, recovery and management. Fitness coaches and sports psychologists improve their matching trait-training XP by 5%, 10% or 15%, without an instant trait or base OVR increase. Three staff slots, one person per role. Hiring costs a signing fee plus the first month's salary; further salaries are paid in advance on the monthly signing anniversary. Signing fees are now 10 times higher, with coach and physio salaries increased 10 times and manager salaries 4 times; bonuses are unchanged. Existing careers use the new salary at the next payment, without recharging the signing fee or paid periods. Unpaid contracts end automatically, and dismissal stops the bonus without a refund. Staff and payment dates persist in career saves.

Career ⏳ Season Archive & Development

Permanent year-end summaries compare your OOM rank, base OVR, match average, checkout rate, singles titles and gross tournament earnings. Switch chart metrics and revisit previous seasons. Fully tracked years award Player of the Year, Rookie of the Year and Most Improved using recorded results, with no gameplay bonuses. Summaries close before January ranking expiry and ageing. Missing historical data stays unknown; partial seasons and estimated checkouts are labelled.

Career 📰 Darts World News

A career news feed reports actual upsets, tournament champions, young talents reaching main OOM semi-finals and changes at the top of the main Order of Merit. Includes player and AI results, category filters, unread markers and links to player profiles. Upsets use base OVR; qualifiers are excluded. Keeps the latest 180 stories in your save without inventing past events or affecting gameplay.

Sponsors 🤝 Seasonal Sponsor Goals

Regular contracts now include up to three seasonal goals per year, with optional addenda for existing sponsors. Track official singles wins, a main OOM quarter-final or a year-end ranking target. Bonuses are shown before acceptance and paid at the year change, even after a contract expires. Each completed goal adds 5% to new regular-sponsor offers next year, capped at 15%. No penalties, OVR changes or ranking money; only results after acceptance count toward match and tournament goals. New goals are available through 30 November.
